Methods of Payment

Imperial Housing Agent offers secure, transparent and flexible payment solutions. Our payment plans are designed to make home ownership simple, affordable and convenient.

Accepted Methods of Payment

Bank Transfer

Direct deposits to our official company bank account. Secure, fast and recommended for all property purchases.

M-Pesa Paybill

Convenient mobile payments for booking fees, deposits and installment payments. Official details are provided upon approval.

Banker's Cheque

Accepted for clients purchasing property through mortgage financing or corporate investments.
